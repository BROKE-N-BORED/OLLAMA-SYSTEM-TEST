# Worked methods

Short, reliable methods for the kinds of problems the test
asks about. Each method is stated once, plainly.

## Percentages

To find p percent of N: multiply N by p, then divide by 100.
Example: 15 percent of 240 is 240 times 15 = 3600, divided
by 100 = 36.

## Distance, speed, time

Distance = speed times time. Speed = distance divided by
time. Time = distance divided by speed. Keep the units
consistent before multiplying.

## Averages

An average is the total divided by the count. If values are
missing, rebuild the total first: total = average times
count.

## Sharing equally

Each share is the total divided by the number of shares.
Check by multiplying the share back by the count.

## Area and perimeter

Rectangle area = length times width. Rectangle perimeter =
2 times (length + width). Square area = side times side.

## Solving ax + b = c

Subtract b from both sides, then divide both sides by a.
Check by substituting the answer back into the equation.

## Sequences

Look at the differences between consecutive terms first.
A constant difference means add it each time; a constant
ratio means multiply. Differences of differences reveal
squared patterns.

## Word problems with money

List every charge separately (starting fee, per-mile or
per-item rate times the count, tip or tax), then add the
list once, carefully, column by column.
